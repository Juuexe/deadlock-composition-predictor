    Methodology and limitations

    Match compositions and completed outcomes come from the independent Deadlock API. The predictor uses hero choices only, without accounts, ranks, personal histories, items or live gameplay. Hero appearances describe training support, not evidence for this exact matchup.

    The active model learns regularized hero associations from earlier matches. It was compared on later matches without retraining. Accuracy measures winner picking; log loss and Brier score measure probability quality. Lower probability scores are better. Availability sampling, hourly API caps and uncertain patch boundaries limit generalization. A 58% estimate is not a guarantee or proof of precisely calibrated 58% odds.

    Draft assistance samples uniformly from remaining supported heroes. It is an experimental ranking under that assumption, not a validated counter-pick or evidence that choosing a hero causes a win.

    Automatic match-start detection is deferred: the public active-match endpoint covers only the top 200 watch-tab matches, so it cannot guarantee detection of every player's match within five seconds.
